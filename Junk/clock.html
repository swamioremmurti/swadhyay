Solar Sandhyā Calculator

No time zones, no libraries — pure JS. Uses Local Solar Time (LMST) derived from your latitude/longitude.

☀️Outputs in Local Solar Time (no time zone).
Tip: West longitudes are negative. For example, New Delhi ≈ lat 28.6139, lon 77.2090.
Definitions used:
  • Brahma Muhūrta: [Sunrise − 1h36] → [Sunrise − 48m] (48 min duration).
  • Prātaḥ Sandhyā: [Sunrise − 48m] → [Sunrise + 48m].
  • Mādhyāhnika: [Solar Noon − 24m] → [Solar Noon + 24m].
  • Sāyam (Āsura) Sandhyā: [Sunset − 12m] → [Sunset + 48m].
